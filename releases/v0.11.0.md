# v0.11.0 — Second volcano and Lava King

Fire continues beyond the labyrinth: walk back outdoors, jump over rolling stones on a second volcano, and fight the Lava King at its summit. The upright animated boss follows the player's drawing, with his square helmet, red collar, long arms, fiery sword and shield.

The Lava King has ten hearts. His shield blocks punches from the front; sword and fireball attacks have visible windups and can be jumped. Land punches during recovery or attack from behind. Defeat him to unlock the final Fire portal. The new climb keeps slope-aligned four-paw jumps and the camera's forward view, with checkpoints along the mountain.

Validation: all 17 Node suites pass. The second climb and a full ten-heart encounter were completed without damage at 30, 60 and 120 FPS. Additional checks cover the labyrinth exit, phone/desktop framing, touch jumping, shield direction, attack warnings, immunity, hit stun, checkpoints, restart and defeat-gated completion.
