# v0.13.5 — Ship cabin air and the tide portal key

The ship air pockets align with the painted cabins. Their walls, roofs, portholes and treasure remain visible above the waterline, replacing the large opaque rectangle. Side hatches connect the hold directly to each cabin waterline, with painted timber edges and matching physical deck gaps.

A hand-painted golden key hangs just above the first cabin's waterline. The bunny can swim up the left hatch, breathe with its head above water, and swim right to collect it. The HUD shows whether the ship key has been found. It survives losing a heart and respawning; restarting the run restores the collectible.

The final tide portal remains closed until the ship key is collected. Its locked appearance and nearby prompt point back to the ship cabin; collecting the key opens the portal and allows the level to finish.
