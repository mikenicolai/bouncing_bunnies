# v0.12.6 — Slope movement and flying Lava King

Four-paw artwork is now used only while grounded on a real incline. Flat stretches after the labyrinth and at the summits stay upright, and every jump uses the upright painted poses with or without the quiver. Slope-relative jump physics still lets the bunny clear rolling stones.

Equipped arrows now damage labyrinth zombies, stop at maze walls and use the same collision path as the aiming guide. The bow points vertically when climbing up/down; Z still draws/stows it. Shift with the movement keys increases outdoor speed from 260 to 360 and labyrinth speed from 165 to 230. Touch movement keeps its existing pace.

The Lava King fires ground shots at 330 rather than 255, with a 0.55-second casting tell. He also rises, crosses the arena, fires three aimed shots during each flight and lands. His moving hitbox follows the painted artwork, and arrow hits during flight do not cancel the movement. Ground shield and sword attacks, ten hearts, checkpoints and portal completion remain active.

The fire camera places the ground at roughly 80% of the viewport height, leaving space above for flight. At the summit it frames the bunny and boss together when they fit in the view, while keeping the bunny visible on narrower screens.

Validation: all 21 Node suites pass. Added coverage checks flat/slope stance boundaries, upright rolling-stone jumps, Shift release/blur and ladder movement, arrows in both directions and vertically, wall occlusion, camera height at desktop/portrait sizes, three-shot flight and landing, airborne boss hits, pause/respawn, and a full ten-heart flying fight at 30/60/120 FPS.
