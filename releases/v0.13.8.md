# v0.13.8 — Five arrows and rare magic

7 October 2026 · Build 2026100703

- The bow pickup now chooses a reachable labyrinth corridor on every new Fire run, avoiding the previous location and both doors. Checkpoint recovery keeps the location.
- Five painted arrow slots appear at the top of the game. Each shot spends an arrow; one spent slot refills every three seconds of active play. The next arrow is underlined, and a countdown and progress line show the next return. Stowing still recharges; pausing freezes it.
- Initial and returned arrows independently roll 89% normal (one heart), 10% fire (two hearts), or 1% rainbow (three hearts). Both zombies and the Lava King take the matching damage in one hit, preserving shields and stun windows.
- Added original transparent normal, flaming and rainbow artwork with the built-in ImageGen tool, used in the HUD and projectiles. The artwork, all three arrow types and an animated recharge demo are available in `previews/arrow-upgrade-review.html`.

Validation: exact probability boundaries, varied pickup reachability, five-shot limit, three-second sequential returns, stow/pause/checkpoint/reset behavior, real projectile damage and shields at 30/60/120 FPS; limited-ammo boss victories; Fire route, controls, mobile layout and painted movement regressions. Browser inspection of desktop and portrait-phone inventories.
