# v0.10.0 — Volcano paws and rolling relics

The Fire volcano is now a jumping challenge: the bunny runs on four paws and jumps over small painted stones rolling downhill. Jump height accounts for uphill movement, landing follows the slope, and keyboard, touch button and upward joystick input all work. Stone contact costs one life with normal immunity; the summit leaves room for a safe final approach.

The fiery ground now forms a solid earthy mountain with roots, pebbles and buried antique bunny relics. A new bunny logo has fire, water, wind and earth in its four corners and appears in the page header, title card, favicon and Apple Home Screen icon. The earlier harmless grounded trees, zombie movement through coins and hidden Air wind-gate island remain included.

Validation: all 16 Node test suites pass. Movement and stone avoidance were checked at 30/60/120 FPS at phone and desktop widths. The actual game renderer was visually reviewed in Chrome through a local HTTP server. Physical iPhone installation has not been tested.

Download the game archive, unzip it, and serve the extracted folder with `python3 -m http.server 8080`; then open `http://localhost:8080`. Keep `assets/` and `manifest.webmanifest` beside `index.html`.

Artwork originals and generation prompts: `assets/volcano-artwork-v1.md`. Review scenes: `previews/volcano-paws-review.html`.
