# v0.12.7 — Crater view and labyrinth exit

During the volcano crater fall, the bunny stays about 20% from the top of the screen so the alternating obstacles below are visible earlier. Vertical tracking has no falling lag; the lower ground view returns after landing.

All bunny artwork is displayed at 70% of its previous size, preserving proportions and the foot anchor across running, jumping, slope poses and archery. The bow and quiver shrink with the bunny, and arrows and the aiming guide start at the scaled bow.

The second volcano now starts at the labyrinth's outside edge, aligned to its exit floor, so the terrain no longer covers the final ladder. Climb to the top of the ladder and walk right through the exit corridor.

Validation: all 21 Node test suites pass, including crater framing at desktop/portrait sizes and 30/60/120 FPS, the final ladder-to-ground route, paired artwork and scaled bow placement, zombie arrows, and the complete ten-heart Lava King fight. The live game was checked visually in the browser.
