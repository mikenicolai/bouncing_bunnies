# v0.12.1 — Stow the arrow and jump again

The extra STOW/EQUIP button and E key now work in the labyrinth, on the second volcano and in the Lava King arena. Put the arrow away to restore normal movement and jumping on the hills and summit. Pressing HIT while it is stowed punches instead of silently drawing the bow. Fired arrows keep flying, and checkpoint respawns preserve your equipped or stowed choice.

The bunny carries the arrow visibly on its back during normal standing, running, jumping, crouching and punches. Four-paw carrying poses are used on the volcano slopes. Up/down continues to climb labyrinth ladders with the arrow equipped or stowed.

Validation: all 18 Node suites pass, including real keyboard/touch toggles, restored jumping, no accidental firing, carried-arrow rendering across normal poses, ladder climbs in both weapon states at 30/60/120 FPS, and complete ten-heart ranged fights.
