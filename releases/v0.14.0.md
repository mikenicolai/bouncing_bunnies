# v0.14.0 — Tempest and glowing wind stars

7 October 2026 · Build 2026100705

Tempest begins with ten hearts. His painted wind shots can be jumped over or ducked under. At five hearts he visibly recharges once to seven, then moves faster, fires paired gusts more often and summons up to two cloud monsters. Defeating him clears the storm and opens the downdraft passage.

Five glowing, painted wind ninja stars are tucked on a visible, unmarked island beneath the summit. Turn back and drop off the left edge to find them, then jump back via the blue cloud. X/F throws; Z equips or stows, and jumping remains available. Misses and shield deflections settle on solid clouds and can be collected again. Monster hits also leave stars recoverable. A successful boss hit embeds a star permanently. Five hits defeat Tempest, including the interrupted second hit that triggers his recharge.

The actual-game practice page is `previews/tempest-wind-stars.html`. It includes the hidden ledge, first and second boss phases, miss recovery and paused artwork. The new star PNG preserves generated transparency; its exact built-in imagegen prompt is saved beside the asset.
