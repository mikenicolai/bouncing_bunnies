# v0.12.2 — Four-paw running and Z to stow

Z now equips or stows the arrow, replacing E. The extra STOW/EQUIP touch button continues to work in the labyrinth, on the second climb and in the Lava King arena.

With the arrow stowed, outdoor and summit movement uses the full four-paw cycle: four running poses and takeoff, airborne and landing poses. The arrow stays attached to the bunny’s back, follows the hill angle and remains visible through the jump. Equipped bow movement still uses the standing aiming poses; labyrinth ladder movement and punching remain available.

The animation review now opens with a looping run and jump and an enlarged view taken from the real game canvas. Choose the uphill preview to inspect slope-aligned jumps. Both previews use the game’s physics and renderer.

Validation: all 19 Node suites pass, including Z/E key behavior, repeat protection, all four gait frames, three jump phases, stowed-arrow rendering, repeated safe preview loops, touch controls, labyrinth climbing and ranged boss fights.
