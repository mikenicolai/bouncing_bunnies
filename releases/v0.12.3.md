# v0.12.3 — Upright running on two legs

Normal movement now uses a new eight-pose upright running cycle, with bent knees, progressive steps, arm swings and subtle ear movement. The bunny runs on two hind feet while its forearms stay off the ground. The cycle works facing either direction, with or without the arrow. Z continues to equip or stow the arrow.

The stowed arrow stays visible on the bunny’s back through running, idle, jumping and punches. On the hills, the support foot follows the slope while the torso stays upright; the jump animation reads velocity relative to the hill for the correct airborne pose. Existing jumping, crouching, punching, ladder movement and equipped aiming poses remain available.

The animation review opens with an enlarged looping upright run and jump. Choose the uphill loop to inspect the foot alignment. New artwork and the final built-in ImageGen prompts are saved in `assets/pink-ear-bunny-upright-run-v1.png` and its companion Markdown file.

Validation: all 20 Node suites pass, covering eight distinct atlas cells, uniform body size, left/right facing, planted feet and attached arrow on flat ground and slopes, upright torso, jump/crouch/punch/aiming selection, safe preview loops and complete ranged boss fights.
