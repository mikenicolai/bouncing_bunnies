# v0.12.8 — Painted crater and labyrinth connection

The volcano rim, both crater walls, the alternating descent ledges and the landing platforms now use the fiery earth artwork. The gray cavern blocks have been replaced with painted red clay, roots, basalt and buried relics.

A new painted stone-to-earth transition joins the labyrinth's last solid column to the second volcano. Weathered plum-gray masonry breaks into volcanic soil, roots and rubble. The painting is clipped to solid wall cells below the exit floor, keeping the final ladder and exit corridor clear.

The original generated RGBA asset and both generation prompts are saved in `assets/labyrinth-ember-transition-v1.png` and `assets/labyrinth-ember-transition-v1.md`. Created and refined with the built-in ImageGen tool.

Validation: all 21 Node test suites pass. The crater rim, live descent and labyrinth join were visually checked in the browser. Movement, collision geometry, the high descent camera and combat are unchanged.
