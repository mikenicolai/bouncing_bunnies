# v0.14.2 — Phone playfield and separate control dock

8 October 2026 · Build 2026100801

On portrait phones, touch controls sit on an opaque gray dock below the game. Land is near 86% of the actual playing field height, revealing more sky and upcoming platforms. Starting soil reaches the dock without a cutaway gap at the left screen edge. Landscape phones use a compact bottom dock; desktop framing remains unchanged.

World rendering is clipped above the dock, and Air, Earth, Water and Fire cameras use the playable height. The volcano drop still places the bunny near the top so approaching ledges stay visible. Rotation releases held inputs and reframes the bunny, while fullscreen fits the available viewport and existing iPhone safe-area padding.

Validation: phone layouts at 320×568, 375×667, 390×844, 430×932 and 844×390; touch target containment, ground framing, ordinary jumps, volcano descent framing, rotation, fullscreen geometry and desktop restoration. Browser checks of portrait/landscape game rendering, plus Air, Water, Fire and painted-bunny regression checks.
