# v0.20.8 — Limited Castle triple jump

Castle now supports a ground jump followed by two quick extra taps, using the same 0.52-second window and velocity cap as Air. A fourth tap has no effect. Landing restores the sequence; Castle monster bounces cannot refill the boost budget. Aiming still requires stowing the bow before jumping.

Validated at 30/60/120 FPS through keyboard, touch and joystick inputs, including held-key repeats, late taps, ledge falls, monster bounces and landing reset. Castle route and bow combat regressions pass; real browser keyboard and portrait/landscape touch checks cover all three taps.
