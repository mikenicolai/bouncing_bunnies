# v0.13.4 — Prominent cacti and heads above water

The opening cactus clusters are about 35–45% wider and 30–46% taller. Their painted bases overlap the foreground sand lip, making them clear obstacles on the path. Collision follows the central and outer paddles; single jumps clear all three clusters at normal speed and with Shift running.

The bunny floats 24 units higher in breathing recesses, exposing its painted head above the waterline while the lower body remains in the water. Oxygen now refills only at the higher head-above-water position. Cave and ship checkpoints use the same raised position, and open-water surface swimming uses it too. Strokes inside recesses still cannot become airborne jumps.

Validation: water, water obstacles, water surface life, full-route, mobile and painted-animation suites pass. Tests cover all three cacti at normal and Shift speed, every cave/ship breathing recess and respawn, and the full underwater route at 30/60/120 FPS. A new breathing boundary check rejects the old submerged-face position. Browser review confirms larger foreground cacti and the bunny's face above the chamber waterline.
