# v0.13.0 — Long water adventure

The water level grows from 4,200 to 11,500 world units, matching the lava level. A longer beach jumping section leads to a continuous sandy slope that gradually descends through the horizontal sea surface, replacing the abrupt blue wall at the shoreline.

The route continues through a kelp grotto, a painted sunken pirate ship with a treasure hold and two air-filled cabins, then a deep underwater cave. There are nine reachable air pockets, 55 gold coins, six sharks and three pirates. Two open ship hatches connect the treasure hold to the cabins; the surrounding deck blocks swimming through the wood. Painted slate-blue cave rocks and sandy seabed artwork follow the terrain, with seaweed, coral, fish and turtles along the route.

Air pockets refill the breath meter and become safe drowning checkpoints. Space gives a swim stroke inside the cave and cabins. Surface leaps remain available in the open sea; the cave roof prevents jumping over the enclosed route. The tide gate finishes the level, leaving the final monster for a future design decision.

Validation: all 22 Node suites pass. The complete swimming route is traversed using ordinary movement controls and oxygen at 30, 60 and 120 FPS, including diving below each reef, entering both ship hatches and reaching every air chamber without losing a life. Traversal fixtures disable enemy damage to isolate movement and breath spacing; separate tests cover shark and pirate combat, shoreline entry, touch swimming, deck collisions and drowning recovery. Browser artwork review covers the shoreline, shipwreck and cave.
