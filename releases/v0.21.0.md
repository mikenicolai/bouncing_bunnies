# v0.21.0 — Three-screen Castle lava tower

The Castle’s inner door now opens into a tower instead of ending the chapter. Twelve alternating stone ledges climb 1,620 world pixels, equal to three standard game screens, above a molten lava pool. Each ledge is reachable with the limited triple jump and holds one collectible coin. Painted masonry, windows and a summit doorway use the existing Castle artwork.

The camera follows vertically, frames the shaft horizontally and preserves the climber’s position when phones rotate. Falling into lava costs one heart and returns Benny to the tower entrance, even during damage immunity. There are no mid-climb checkpoints; collected coins, wallet and bow/ammunition survive the fall. The existing 20-coin heart purchase or five-second test reward remains available at zero hearts. Reaching the summit ends this chapter without claiming victory over the unbuilt final villain.

A dedicated tower practice preview isolates its coins and progress from the normal save.
