# v0.12.0 — Labyrinth arrow and aiming combat

Find a reusable enchanted arrow in the Fire labyrinth before leaving for the second volcano. The bunny visibly carries it through idle, running, jumping and maze combat, then switches to new aiming, walking and firing poses at the summit.

The Lava King now requires ranged attacks. Move left/right, aim up/down and fire with X or F. Aiming disables jumping and shows a dotted flight arc with a target marker. Backing away keeps the bow pointed at the king; arrows can extinguish incoming fireballs. His shield blocks front shots, and recovery opens a chance to remove one of his ten hearts.

The second climb keeps normal jumping until you aim; E or the AIM/WALK touch button changes modes there. The boss arena starts aiming automatically. The arrow survives checkpoint respawns, and a fresh run resets it.

Validation: all 18 Node suites pass, including full ranged fights without jumping or damage at 30/60/120 FPS, reachable pickup and maze exit gating, carry/aim/fire animation states, keyboard/touch controls, preview/projectile agreement, shield direction, swept arrow collisions, fireball interception, checkpoints and reset.
