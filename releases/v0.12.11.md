# v0.12.11 — Continuous lava and smooth enclosed crater descent

The opening lava extends behind all eroded pit edges and down to the bottom of the view, with an animated molten surface. The old rectangular dark background strip is removed.

The crater camera eases into the fall over 1.2 seconds, then stays centred on the shaft while the bunny steers left and right. Narrow screens smoothly zoom out enough to show both walls; HUD and touch controls retain their normal size. Once the entry transition completes, the falling bunny remains at 20% of the screen height. Landing and labyrinth entry use frame-rate-independent camera easing, and leaving the shaft restores the normal view smoothly.

The outer side of the right wall is filled with painted soil instead of leaving an exposed dark margin. Painted earth at the landing extends across the left side of the view to the labyrinth, keeping its entrance open. A muted warm rock background gradually replaces the outdoor sky during the descent. Existing original paintings are reused; no new image assets are needed.

The practice page adds **Enter the crater** and **Crater landing** scenes.

Validation: all 21 Node test suites pass. Added regression checks cover real rim entry without a camera teleport, stationary walls under steering, both walls visible at desktop and portrait widths, 20% screen-height tracking, and reset state at 30/60/120 FPS. The existing damage-free full crater route still passes. Browser checks cover the opening lava, descent, landing and narrow-screen framing.
