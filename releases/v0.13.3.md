# v0.13.3 — Fish routes, surface breathing and a filled beach start

Decorative fish schools bend around stone columns and ship decks, keeping their full bodies and tails clear of solid obstacles, the cave roof and the seabed. Turtles follow the same clearance rules with space for their wider silhouettes.

Sand extends beyond the left side of the starting screen, removing the empty wedge below the beach. The beach-to-seabed connection remains continuous.

Validation: water, water obstacles, full-route, mobile, painted-animation and new water-surface-life suites pass. Surface/respawn checks cover every cave and ship chamber at 30/60/120 FPS.
