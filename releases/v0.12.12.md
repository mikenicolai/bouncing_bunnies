# v0.12.12 — Gentle walk A in the game

Normal upright movement now uses the user's approved gentle walk A: eight complete painted poses, a soft bounce and gently moving pink ears. Its cadence matches the review at normal walking speed. The plain sprite sheet is copied exactly from the approved artwork, and a matching painted quiver variant keeps collected arrows visible on the bunny's back.

Shift still selects the faster upright running animation. Four-paw movement stays restricted to volcano slopes. Upright jumps, crouching, attacks, archery, Z equip/stow, collisions and the 70% artwork scale retain their existing behavior. Each pose is a single bitmap, mirrored uniformly and anchored at its feet.

Validation: 21 Node suites pass, including walking/running selection in all elements, both Shift keys, all eight painted frames in both directions and inventory states, uniform rendering, actual jump/landing sequences at 30/60/120 FPS and slope-only four-paw movement. The plain asset is checked byte-for-byte against the approved A preview; both sprite sheets have eight complete poses and transparent gutters. Browser review covers the live practice game.
