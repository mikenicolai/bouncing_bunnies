The approved eight-pose artwork now drives actual gameplay. Upright running is used on normal terrain and in the labyrinth; volcano slopes use four paws. Running and jumping have matching complete painted versions with and without the quiver. Standing, crouching, punching and bow aiming also keep the quiver consistent after arrow collection.

A playable practice page starts immediately on flat ground, on a volcano slope, or beside the labyrinth arrow. It uses the production game renderer and controls, with hazards cleared for animation testing. The full game keeps normal progression and hazards.
