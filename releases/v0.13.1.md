# v0.13.1 — Cave air recesses, cactus jumps and floating pirates

Cave air chambers now derive their height from the local rock roof. Their waterlines sit at least 30 units above both sides of the opening, inside a shallow rounded recess, with water visible below the line. Air no longer appears suspended beneath the cave ceiling. The ship cabins retain their timber-roof waterlines.

The opening beach is continuous sand with three painted prickly-pear cactus obstacles. A normal-speed jump clears each cactus without requiring a double jump or immunity. The collision shapes follow the taller central paddles and lower spreading base, rather than treating the entire sprite as a tall rectangle.

Skeleton pirates float and sway on fixed-length chains attached to painted iron ballast balls resting on the seabed. Their hook/cutlass attacks and two hearts remain. Chain links and ballast are solid swimming obstacles; defeating a pirate clears its tether. A chain that moves into the bunny nudges it toward a clear side so the movement controls cannot become trapped.

Validation: all 23 Node suites pass. New checks cover raised air waterlines, underwater breathing boundaries, three clean cactus jumps at 30/60/120 FPS, cactus damage, fixed tether lengths, deck clearance, solid chains, release after defeat, and recovery when a moving chain intersects the player. Full-route tests retain the solid pirate tethers, disable enemy damage/weapon swings to isolate traversal, and reach all air pockets and the tide gate at 30/60/120 FPS without losing a life. Separate combat checks cover both pirate weapons and stun behavior. Browser review confirms the cactus course, roof-bound chamber waterline and floating pirate/ball artwork.
