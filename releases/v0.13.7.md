The water level ends at a painted stone arch with a visible brass keyhole and iron grille. Swim toward the lock with the key from the pirate ship’s first air cabin: the key slides into the keyhole, then the grille rises smoothly into the cave roof. The passage stays physically blocked until the lift completes. Swim through to the right to finish.
