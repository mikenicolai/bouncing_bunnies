# v0.12.10 — Volcano shaft crystal hazards and painted pit edges

The four ledges that project into the volcano's falling passage now use a complete painted horizontal crystal formation, mirrored to grow from alternate walls. Their original obstacle footprints and safe passage widths remain intact. The decorative crystal scenery is retained.

Small ground gaps now have natural painted boundaries on both sides. Fire uses ember soil and hanging charred roots; meadow platforms and forest cliffs use mossy turf, stones and woody roots; the shoreline uses sand and sandstone. Rounded underlying silhouettes keep old rectangular corners from showing through the paintings. Air islands retain their existing painted boundaries.

The practice page adds shaft-crystal, fire-pit, meadow-pit, shoreline and forest-cliff scenes, plus a pause/resume button for inspecting the artwork.

Assets: `assets/volcano-shaft-crystals-v1.png`, `assets/grassy-pit-edge-v1.png`, `assets/sandy-pit-edge-v1.png`. Original RGBA paintings and complete prompts are saved in the project; see `assets/shaft-crystals-and-pit-edges-v1.md`. Generated using the built-in ImageGen tool.

Validation: all 21 gameplay test suites passed, including the damage-free alternating crater descent at phone/desktop widths and 30/60/120 FPS, meadow regression, forest route, water route, jumping and archery. Browser visual checks covered the shaft obstacles and painted boundaries.
