# v0.12.9 — Both cliff edges and volcanic crystals

Both sides of the volcano crater now have complete painted corners, eroded rock faces and hanging roots. The left rim has a rounded undercut connecting its overhang to the shaft. The opposite rim uses the mirrored painting, and the exposed faces continue down both crater walls.

The flat triangular background rocks are replaced by painted volcanic crystal formations with muted plum facets, basalt bases and ember seams. The same artwork appears faintly in the cavern background. These are decorative scenery; movement, collision boundaries and the alternating descent gaps are unchanged.

Original generated RGBA paintings and prompts are saved as `assets/ember-cliff-edge-v1.png`, `assets/volcanic-crystal-ridge-v1.png` and `assets/crater-cliffs-and-crystals-v1.md`. Both paintings were made with the built-in ImageGen tool.

Validation: all 21 Node test suites pass, including the damage-free crater route, slope jumping, camera framing, labyrinth route and ranged boss fight. Both summit edges and the exposed descent faces were visually checked in the browser.
