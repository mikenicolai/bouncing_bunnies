# v0.22.1 · Bunny Hop music and helpful adventures

Build 2026111601 · 16 November 2026.

An original, light sixteen-bar tune now accompanies gameplay. Soft plucks, bass and offbeat chords are synthesized locally with Web Audio, with no music download. The existing Sound controls mute music and effects together. Music pauses on maps, death screens and the test ad; background tabs and unfocused windows mute audio. Touch/keyboard gestures enable playback and recover interrupted audio contexts.

Clicking or tapping an unlocked map destination now starts it directly, including replay of completed worlds. Future worlds remain locked. Continue still resumes a paused run.

Neptunus moves and attacks even without the Aqua Suit or trident. An unequipped challenger can retreat, and paid retries return to the earlier checkpoint without granting equipment. A failed unprepared attempt reveals a short hint naming only the missing relics. Nearby pickup glints make the relics easier to find. Collecting both relics and re-entering seals the normal boss arena.

- Production release browser regression: developer tools remain absent, paid continuation, real five-second current-level restart, saving and progression, no page errors or missing resources. Load audit: `previews/production-release-audit-v0.22.1.json`.
- Neptunus unit/browser regression: all equipment combinations, attacks, unarmed hits, tailored failure guidance, fair paid retry, retreat and equipped re-entry; desktop/phone/landscape layouts.
- Mobile controls/layout, coin/revival, developer/save isolation and Wildwood boss unit regressions.

The CrazyGames preparation work listed in v0.22.0 remains outstanding, including download-size reduction and replacing/hiding the simulated advertisement. Audio now handles browser focus, visibility and interrupted contexts; gameplay itself does not automatically pause on blur. Real-device Safari testing and platform SDK integration remain separate release checks.
