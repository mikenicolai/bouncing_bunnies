# v0.20.4 — Coin wallet and checkpoint revives

Build 2026101301 · 13 October 2026.

- Collected sun-coins credit a shared wallet that persists across levels, restarts and reloads. The run's perfect-score counter remains separate.
- At zero hearts, spend 20 coins to continue with one heart, wait through a clearly labeled five-second test ad for three hearts, or restart the level.
- Revival keeps collected coins, score, equipment, ammunition, completed worlds and enemy/boss health, returning to the current checkpoint with damage immunity and cleared attacks.
- The simulated ad freezes gameplay and input. Purchases and rewards apply once; insufficient funds disable buying. No real ad service or SDK is connected.

Checks cover all collection/death paths and gameplay at 30/60/120 FPS, persistence and blocked storage, price/timing, preserved progress, stale callbacks, desktop and portrait/landscape phone controls. Review fixtures keep test coins out of the player's real wallet.
