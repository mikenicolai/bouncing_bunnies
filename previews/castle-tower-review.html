Castle · lava tower

Climb three game screens above the lava. Jump right over lava, then back left. Tap twice quickly or pull the joystick strongly upward for a high jump. Narrow ledges crack on contact and collapse after about one second—keep moving. O chooses Bow, Trident, Knives or Stars; tap their icons to choose directly. Lava costs a heart and returns you to the entrance. Play the full game
