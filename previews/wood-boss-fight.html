The Wildwood final fight

Five hearts on the small monster, eight on the giant. Find three throwing knives below the last trees in the full level. This practice fight starts with all three. X / F throws; Z stows them to punch the small monster. Each knife returns after five seconds. The giant needs eight knife hits. Move with the arrows, jump with Space. Jump over earthquake waves and leave the red patch at your feet before mountains grow. Jump or throw a ready knife to break the vine grab. The normal route reaches this arena after the last woodland branch.
