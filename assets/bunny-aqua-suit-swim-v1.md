# Painted cyber Aqua suit and animation

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool from the approved cyber Aqua suit concept, the original painted Bunny, and the existing swimming sheet. Original generated PNGs are copied unchanged into the project:

- `bunny-aqua-suit-swim-v1.png`: transparent 1774 × 887 atlas, four columns by two rows. Frames 0–3 alternate paws and flipper kicks; 4–5 dive; 6 winds up; 7 punches. The runtime registers the face center in each frame, uses one scale, and mirrors for leftward swimming. The equipped suit also stays visible during a surface leap.
- `aqua-suit-pickup-v1.png`: matching empty painted suit for the ruins pedestal.

The maintained `water-ruins-v1.js` module and its embedded copy use the new painted frames after collection, retain the original render as a loading fallback, and keep the held trident separate. Existing swimming controls, hit boxes, breathing, pickups and combat mechanics are unchanged. `previews/aqua-suit-animation.html` shows an enlarged animation and the actual swimming level. `previews/aqua-suit-swimming.gif` is a two-second loop of 40 frames captured from that same Canvas renderer; the generated sprite artwork is preserved unchanged.

## Swimming atlas prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production transparent 2D sprite sheet for the Bouncing Bunnies swimming level. Input images: Image 1 defines EXACT bunny character identity and our existing softly painted cartoon game style. Image 2 is the existing swimming animation and defines pose language only. Image 3 defines the approved Aqua suit equipment design only; simplify that concept to fit Image 1's softer, rounder, less detailed painted game style. Request: the SAME small cream-white Bunny wearing the approved cyber Aqua suit in EIGHT genuine different animation poses. Preserve original rounded short body, long pink inner ears, simple vertical BLACK eyes, big happy black mouth with exactly ONE square white tooth, bold slightly organic dark outline, soft cream fur and painted shading. Suit design in every pose: rounded teal armor with graphite flexible joints, simple silver cuffs, thin bright cyan circuit seams, one circular warm golden chest core, compact breathing pack, teal flipper boots, clear bubble helmet and two clear flexible ear sleeves enclosing visible pink ears. Face must remain clearly visible. No tall human anatomy, no muscular armor, no realistic 3D. Reduce concept art's mechanical detail so equipment is readable on a tiny game sprite. EXACT atlas layout: FOUR equal square cells across by TWO rows, landscape 2:1 aspect ratio. Full character entirely inside each cell with at least 10 percent genuinely transparent margin on all edges, NO overlaps, uniform character scale and consistent head and body-center registration per action. All characters face RIGHT. Top row reading order: frame 0 horizontal swimming, near paw reaching forward and far paw pulling back, flippers scissored with near flipper up; frame 1 horizontal swimming, near paw pulling under chest, far paw reaching ahead, near flipper down and far flipper up; frame 2 horizontal swimming, near paw backward, far paw forward and down, feet in the opposite kick to frame 0; frame 3 horizontal swimming, near paw recovering forward under chin, far paw pulling back, opposite kick to frame 1. These four poses form a smooth continuous paddle-and-flipper cycle; keep helmet center in exactly the same relative place across the four cells, only limbs and softly bent ear sleeves change. Bottom row: frame 4 downward-right dive, both paws reaching down-right, one flipper bending behind; frame 5 matching downward-right dive with opposite flipper kick; frame 6 horizontal underwater punch windup, near fist pulled back beside shoulder; frame 7 horizontal underwater punch extended RIGHT with near fist out in front. Suit remains identical throughout. Transparent background, isolated complete character in each cell. NO water, scenery, bubbles, labels, grid, text, checkerboard, floor shadows, weapons, trident, additional characters, or watermark.

## Pickup prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: painted transparent Aqua suit pickup icon for the Bouncing Bunnies swimming level. Reference image 1 defines the FINAL suit design and exact simplified painted game style. Reference 2 is the original concept for the same equipment. Create ONE empty wearable Aqua suit floating upright, full suit from ear chambers to flipper boots visible, front three-quarter view facing slightly right, centered with generous 12 percent transparent margins. It is the exact rounded teal-and-graphite cyber diving suit seen on Bunny in reference 1: simple silver joint cuffs, short rounded gloves, large teal flipper boots, small cyan circuit seams and glowing cuff accents, warm circular golden chest core, compact breathing pack. At the top is a completely CLEAR empty bubble helmet with TWO clear long rounded flexible ear sleeves sized for bunny ears, with silver rims and cyan earpiece. Helmet and ear sleeves must be visibly EMPTY transparent glass, with NO face, eyes, mouth, fur, bunny or pink ears inside. Keep proportions short, round, cute, thick organic dark outlines and soft painted shading matching reference 1. The isolated suit must be simple and readable as a small collectible. Genuinely transparent background. No scenery, water, bubbles, pedestal, ground shadow, text, labels, grid, checkerboard, extra objects or watermark.
